Navigate to the Register Page, fill in your Full Name, Email, Student ID/Roll Number, select your academic program, provide a mobile number, create a secure password (at least 6 characters), and submit. You can immediately log in with your credentials.
Once logged in, go to the Student Dashboard. You will see your overall attendance percentage, cumulative CGPA, and a breakdown table of marks obtained across all semester courses pulled directly from the central database.
Visit the Events Page to view all scheduled events, dates, venues, and descriptions. Click the "Register Now" button next to any event. The system will save your participation via AJAX and mark it as registered in your profile.
StudentHub uses industry best practices: passwords are encrypted using BCrypt one-way hashing (`password_hash`), all database queries use PDO prepared statements to completely mitigate SQL Injection, HTML output is sanitized against XSS, and role-based access control prevents unauthorized access to administrative endpoints.
